Business Project Proposal

Company: Nordblick Solutions GmbH
Project: Digital Document Management System

The company plans to introduce a new digital document management system
for its internal departments.

The total project budget is 250,000 EUR.

The planned project start date is September 1, 2026.
The expected completion deadline is December 15, 2026.

The main objectives are:
- Reduce manual document processing
- Improve access to internal information
- Automate repetitive administrative tasks

Potential risks:
- Delays during system integration
- Employee training requirements
- Data protection and access control issues

Project Manager: Anna Becker

The project is currently in the planning phase.
